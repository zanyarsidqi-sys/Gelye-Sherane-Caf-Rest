# Phase 3 — Favorite Workspaces

- Authorized sections can be starred from the searchable management list.
- Starred sections appear in a dedicated My favorites area on the home workspace.
- Favorite choices are limited to the current in-memory session and are **not** yet synced or persisted across devices.
- No database migrations, RLS policies, authentication, or pricing logic were changed.
- Requires Flutter analyze and interactive tests on Windows and mobile layout.
