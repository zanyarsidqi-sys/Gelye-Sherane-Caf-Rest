# Upgrade 420 — Customer Consent Outreach Tracking

Adds a permission-controlled, append-only-by-API outreach log for identity review records. Staff can record channel, note, and timestamp and inspect outreach history. It explicitly **does not capture or verify customer consent**, does not activate accounts, and does not enable credit payment. Requires `20261010_420_credit_consent_outreach.sql`. Flutter compilation and live Supabase tests were not performed.
